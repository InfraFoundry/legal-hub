Privacy Policy for RepoLynx Media Publisher

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RepoLynx Media Publisher, operated under the InfraFoundry project namespace, handles information when connecting to and publishing content on authorized social media accounts, including GitPulse channels.

RepoLynx Media Publisher is intended to operate only with accounts explicitly authorized by their owners or administrators.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on permissions granted by an authorized user, the application may process:

  • account, profile, Page, business, or channel identifiers;
  • public profile or Page information available through the platform API;
  • granted permissions and authorization status;
  • access tokens and related authentication metadata;
  • authorized publication content such as text, captions, links, images, videos, and media URLs;
  • publication identifiers, delivery status, timestamps, API errors, and retry information;
  • technical and security logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, protect, and audit the service.

We do not ask users to provide social media passwords, two-factor authentication secrets, recovery codes, or payment card information.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used only to:

  • authenticate an authorized account connection;
  • identify selected accounts, profiles, Pages, or channels;
  • prepare, schedule, publish, or manage authorized content;
  • receive publication status and platform identifiers;
  • prevent duplicate or unauthorized publication;
  • diagnose errors and maintain service security;
  • comply with applicable legal and platform requirements;
  • process access revocation and data deletion requests.

4. Legal Site Visitors

This legal website is a static GitHub Pages site. InfraFoundry does not add analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, forms, or behavioral tracking.

GitHub may process limited technical information necessary to deliver GitHub Pages under GitHub's own terms and privacy practices. InfraFoundry does not receive a visitor analytics dashboard from this site.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to the social platform selected by the authorized user because the transmission is necessary to perform the requested publication or account-management operation.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as needed to host, secure, or operate the service. We may disclose information when required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or to protect users, the service, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

6. Access Tokens and Credentials

Access tokens and credentials are not published on this legal website.

Production credentials must be stored outside public source code and protected with appropriate secret-management controls. Access is limited to authorized systems and personnel according to operational need.

Users may revoke access through the relevant Meta, Facebook, Instagram, or Threads account settings. Revocation may prevent further automated publication.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide authorized functionality, maintain security, resolve failures, comply with applicable obligations, or establish and defend legal claims.

Access tokens are retained until they expire, are revoked, are replaced, the connected account is removed, or deletion is requested, subject to technical and legal requirements.

Publication records and security logs may be retained for operational, audit, anti-abuse, and troubleshooting purposes and are deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

8. Data Deletion

Users may revoke access through platform settings and request deletion by following:

RepoLynx User Data Deletion Instructions

Until a Meta Data Deletion Callback is implemented, the support email listed below remains the working mechanism for deletion requests.

A deletion request must not contain a password, access token, two-factor authentication code, or recovery code.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should promptly revoke access and contact us if they believe an account connection or credential has been compromised.

10. Children's Privacy

RepoLynx Media Publisher is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user's country. Where applicable, processing is performed subject to relevant legal and contractual safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, platform requirements, or applicable law changes. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.
