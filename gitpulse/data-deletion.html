GitPulse User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 25, 2026

You may request deletion of applicable information under the actual control of GitPulse or its RepoLynx publishing infrastructure.

Step 1 — Revoke Platform Access

If you administer a connected GitPulse account or channel, remove or revoke RepoLynx Media Publisher in the connected-app, business-integration, or permissions settings of the relevant platform.

Revocation stops future authorized API operations after it is recognized by the platform and service.

Step 2 — Send a Request

Email: kodo.app.labs@gmail.com
Subject: GitPulse Data Deletion Request

Until a Meta Data Deletion Callback is implemented, this support email is the working request mechanism.

Include:

  • affected platform;
  • public username, profile name, Page name, channel name, or publication URL;
  • brief description of the interaction, connection, or data to delete.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, 2FA codes, recovery codes, private keys, or payment information.

Step 3 — Verification

We may request limited additional information to verify authorization or identify the relevant data. We will never request a social media password or recovery code.

Step 4 — Processing

After verification, applicable identifiers, connection records, messages, interaction records, or publication records that are no longer required will be deleted or anonymized when they are under actual GitPulse or RepoLynx control.

Deleting a comment or message stored directly by a social platform may require action through that platform's settings or support. GitPulse can delete only data under its actual control.

Information may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or defense of legal claims.

Step 5 — Confirmation

We aim to acknowledge a valid request within 7 days and complete applicable deletion within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or technical constraints.

Confirmation or an explanation of information outside our control or retained for a valid reason will be sent to the requester's email.

Technical Notice

This page provides user-facing deletion instructions. It is not an automated platform data deletion callback endpoint and cannot receive server-to-server requests.
